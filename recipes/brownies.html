Brownies

Description

Seven ingredient brownie mix that will dazzle your friends guaranteed.

Ingredients

  • 2c granulated sugar
  • 4 eggs at room temperature
  • 1c salted butter, melted
  • 0.67c cocoa powder
  • 0.75c all-purpose flour
  • 0.5tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.25tsp salt
  • 1c semi-sweet choclate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350F and line 9x9 metal baking dish.
  2. Mix sugar and eggs until sugar is dissolved.
  3. Add butter and vanilla and stir again.
  4. Add flour, cocoa powder and salt and fold until thick.
  5. Fold in choco chips.
  6. Bake 33-38 minutes, use the toothpick test to check.
  7. Let cool for 30 minutes before serving.
