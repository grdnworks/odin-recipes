Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

Quick, 30 minute start to finish recipe for delicious chocolate chip cookies that need no mixer. Perfect for a lazy Sunday.

Ingredients

  • 1 stick butter
  • 0.5c white sugar
  • 0.5c dark brown sugar
  • 1 egg (room temperature)
  • 1tsp vanilla extract
  • 0.5tsp kosher salt
  • 0.5tsp baking soda
  • 1.33c all purpose flour
  • 1c chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Brown butter in a skillet for about 5 minutes at medium heat until it begins to clarify and have brown bits at the bottom.
  2. Wait 5 minutes for butter to cool. Preheat oven for 350F
  3. In a large mixing bowl mix the butter with both sugars until consistent.
  4. Crack in the egg and stir again.
  5. Add the vanilla extract, salt and baking soda. Mix again thoroughly.
  6. Add the flour and choclate chips and mix until flour is no longer visible.
  7. Scoop into roughly 8 balls on lined parchment paper baking sheet.
  8. Bake for 10-13 minutes, look for browning on top.
  9. Let it cool for 5 minutes before serving.
